# 🐍 Snake Game

A classic snake game built with vanilla JavaScript, HTML, and CSS. Control the snake, eat food, grow longer, and try to beat your high score without hitting the walls or yourself!


## Features

-  **Dynamic Speed**: Snake gets faster as you eat more food
-  **High Score Tracking**: Your best score is saved locally
-  **Timer**: Track how long you survive
-  **Responsive Design**: Adapts to different screen sizes
-  **Dual Controls**: Use arrow keys or WASD

## How to Play

1. Use **Arrow Keys** or **WASD** to control the snake's direction
2. Eat the red food blocks to grow and increase your score
3. Avoid hitting the walls or running into yourself
4. Try to beat your high score!
5. When game over, press **SPACE** to restart
